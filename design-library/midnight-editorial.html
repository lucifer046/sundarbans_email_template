Dear Sundarbans,

This Saturday we are keeping it simple. Read a ghazal you love, sing one if you like, or tell us why a certain line stayed with you. You can also just sit in and listen. Cameras are optional.

Hindi, Urdu, English, or any language you write in is welcome.

How the evening runs

01 Opening set. Two members start us off with pieces they picked.
02 Open floor. Raise your hand in the call and take the mic for up to five minutes.
03 Last word. We close with one piece chosen by everyone in the chat.
