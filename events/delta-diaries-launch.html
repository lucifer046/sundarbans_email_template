SUNDARBANS HOUSE THE DELTA DIARIES EST. 2026 • VOL. I
 
Where Connections Became Memories  •  One House, Many Stories

“Every great house writes its history — ours is finally in print.”

We are proud to present The Delta Diaries — Vol. I, Sundarbans House’s first-ever Annual Chronicle. This magazine is more than a document; it is a monument to every member, every organiser, every late night, and every standing ovation we earned together.

From the heat of technical hackathons to the roar of esports arenas, from the sweat of sporting grounds to the grace of cultural stages — and across the grandeur of Saavan, Margazhi, and Paradox — this is the complete story of how Sundarbans kept its name high.

As the 2025–26 House Council tenure draws to a beautiful close, we wanted to leave behind something timeless. This chronicle is our heartfelt tribute to the house that gave us the space to lead, the freedom to learn, and the courage to grow. It is our final “thank you,” and our most cherished way of giving back to the legacy of Sundarbans.
