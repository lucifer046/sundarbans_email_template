Rang Ras Rhythm (RRR)

“Mehfil ek, ehsaas chaar”

Dear Students,

The Sundarbans House Cultural Community is bringing you Rang Ras Rhythm (RRR), a singing competition where every song tells a story and every voice carries an emotion.

Inspired by the four core emotions · joy, sadness, love, and nostalgia · RRR is your chance to express yourself through music and share the feelings behind your favourite song.

Whether your voice carries happiness, touches hearts with sadness, celebrates love, or brings back beautiful memories, we want to hear your story.

EMOTIONS · BHAV
The Four Thematic Emotions
Joy
Khushi · Ullas
Upbeat, energetic tracks celebrating happiness, festivities, and pure delight.
 
Sadness
Dard · Sukoon
Soulful, reflective melodies that touch hearts and express poignant feelings.
Love
Pyaar · Ishq
Romantic, tender songs celebrating affection, devotion, and deep connections.
 
Nostalgia
Yaadein · Ehsaas
Timeless melodies and retro classics that evoke golden memories and days past.
