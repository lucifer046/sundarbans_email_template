Sunday · 8 November 2026

Dear Sundarbans,

Somewhere tonight a laptop is closing, and we are happy about it.

We know what this term looked like. Quizzes at odd hours. Assignments finished at 2 AM. Calls where half of you kept the camera off and the other half were eating dinner. Some of you study after work, some between classes, some after everyone else has gone to sleep. You made it work anyway, and we noticed.

So tonight, give yourself the evening off. No notes, no deadlines. Just a little light.
 
 Wishes for tonight
 

For the 2 AM version of you

You know the one. Cold tea, forty tabs open, a deadline staring back. Tonight, may that hour go to a long chat and a bad joke instead. The submission can wait until morning.

For your plate

May it never run empty. May someone at the table say “just one more” like they mean it, and may you finally give in.

For wherever you are tonight

A hostel corridor, a rented flat, a train seat, a night shift. Wherever it is, may one small light find you there. If it still feels too quiet, message the house. Someone will reply.

Tonight’s sky, from all of us

One small request. Light a diya, even a tiny one, and send us a photo. Rangoli, sweets, a messy balcony, whatever your corner looks like tonight. Drop it in the short form below and we will share a few on our Instagram page.

See you on the calls soon, with a little more light in the room.
