Region

Role & Responsibilities

As a GHC member, you will:
✦ Stay active on GSpaces and WhatsApp groups and support student queries.
✦ Promote House events and activities.
✦ Share ideas and take initiative in organising sessions and regional meetups.
✦ Connect students, especially from smaller towns and less-represented regions.
✦ Work with the Regional Coordinators and House Secretary to strengthen the student community.
Growth & Recognition

Benefits
◆ Official Appreciation Certificate from Sundarbans House.
◆ Recognition as a core Lower House Council team member.
◆ Leadership, event management, and community-building experience.
◆ Opportunity to contribute to House planning and initiatives.

Time & Dedication

Commitment: ~2–3 hours/day on average, depending on activities.
