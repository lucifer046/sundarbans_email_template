Pick Your Team

Our Core Team consists of six departments:

 

Graphic Design

Design posters, event creatives, awareness campaigns, social media visuals, branding assets, and other visual content.
 

WebOps

Manage and maintain the house website, update web content, ensure smooth website functionality, and support the team’s digital presence.
 

Public Relations (PR)

Build and maintain relationships with regional communities, student groups, partner houses, and external organizations; coordinate collaborations, event communication, and public engagement.
 

Outreach

Connect with students, speakers, workshop facilitators, collaborators, and external communities; expand participation, strengthen partnerships, and grow the community network.
 

Video Production

Edit promotional videos, reels, event highlights, workshops, trailers, and other multimedia content while maintaining visual quality and consistency.
 

Social Media & Content

Manage social media platforms, create content, handle content writing, increase audience engagement, and maintain a consistent online presence.

We’re looking for individuals with a genuine interest in Sundarbans House, strong communication and teamwork skills, creativity, reliability, and a willingness to learn. Previous experience is appreciated but not mandatory. Applicants for Graphic Design or Video Production are encouraged to share their portfolio.
