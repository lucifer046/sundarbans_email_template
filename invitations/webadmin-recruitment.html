Selection process

The route from interest to role

01

Expression of Interest

Submit the recruitment form for Sundarbans House. WebOps Committee will review every response and invite selected candidates to the next round.
02

Trial Task

Shortlisted candidates will ship a feature or fix from a supplied problem statement. Submit a pull request from a feature branch, with atomic commits and clear messages.
03

Final Interview

A short interview with WebOps Committee, HoSA, and where applicable, the House Secretary.

Performance framework

How the role is measured

Delivery

Throughput, turnaround by priority, reopen rate, and backlog age.

Reliability

Uptime, recovery time after outages, and deploy health.

Member Rating

House member feedback, collected via a form sent alongside the Secretary and Deputy Secretary feedback forms.

Subjective Feedback

Sundarbans member satisfaction is collected through a feedback form sent to House members alongside the Secretary and Deputy Secretary feedback forms. This provides a subjective measure of how the WebAdmin is perceived by the members they serve.
