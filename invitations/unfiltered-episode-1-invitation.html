ABOUT THE SERIES

What is "Unfiltered"?

"Unfiltered" is our flagship speaker session series designed to bring inspiring personalities to our community for candid, unscripted conversations. We believe that the most valuable lessons come from authentic stories — the failures, the pivots, and the moments of courage that shaped remarkable journeys.

As our inaugural guest, your presence would set the tone for this series and inspire our students to embrace authenticity, pursue their passions, and never stop reinventing themselves.

We would be deeply honored if you could grace this session with your presence. Your insights on resilience, reinvention, and standing up for one's beliefs would be invaluable to our community of aspiring students.

Thank you for taking the time to consider our invitation. We are truly grateful for your willingness to inspire and connect with our community, and we look forward to hosting you.
