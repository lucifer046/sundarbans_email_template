Regional Coordinator Mandate

As the RC for Bengaluru Region, you will hold an official POR within the IITM Student Government and be accountable to the Lower House Council. Your core responsibilities include:

01

Regional Representation & Outreach

Act as the official representative of Sundarbans House across Bengaluru, Karnataka, and Kerala, connecting students to the House Council and the broader IITM Student Government.
 
02

Organise Regional Meetups & Initiatives

Plan and execute regional events, study sessions, and community activities in Bengaluru and key locations across KA & KL under the LHC framework.
 
03

Student Grievance & Support

Address concerns, resolve queries, and serve as a trusted escalation point between regional students and the House Council.
 
04

Foster House Culture & Community Spirit

Champion the values of Sundarbans House in your region, nurturing a strong sense of belonging, identity, and collective pride among members.

Eligibility Criteria

Who Can Apply

No prior experience in student governance is required. This POR is open to all registered students of Sundarbans House residing in or associated with the Bengaluru Region (Karnataka & Kerala) who are proactive, accountable, and genuinely committed to serving their region as a leader within the IITM Student Government.
