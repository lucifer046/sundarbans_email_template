Core Mandate

Key Responsibilities of the Regional Coordinator

 
PILLAR 01 · COUNCIL LIAISON

Official Voice in Lower House Council

Represent student priorities from Karnataka and Kerala directly in the Lower House Council (LHC), attending council proceedings and voting on regional student initiatives.

 
PILLAR 02 · COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

Offline Meetups & Flagship Gatherings

Spearhead offline student meetups, freshers welcomes, celebratory dinners, and informal networking gatherings in Bengaluru, Kochi, Mysuru, and Trivandrum.

 
PILLAR 03 · ACADEMIC SUCCESS

Study Circles & Exam Peer Cohorts

Coordinate course-specific study groups, doubt-clearing sessions, and revision meetups before Quiz 1, Quiz 2, and End-Term examinations.

 
PILLAR 04 · ADVOCACY & SUPPORT

Student Grievance & Welfare Escalation

Act as an approachable, trusted contact for regional peers facing academic or administrative bottlenecks, escalating concerns directly to House Council executives.

Eligibility & Candidacy

Open to all active students of Sundarbans House who reside in or are affiliated with the Bengaluru Region (Karnataka & Kerala).
