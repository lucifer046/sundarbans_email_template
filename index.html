AGENTS.md summary

Email design standards

Every template here follows the master spec in AGENTS.md. The short version:

Writing

  • Names. Sundarbans House, IIT Madras BS Degree. Never "BS Degree Program".
  • Tone. Write like a person on campus. No "dive into", "unlock", "we're thrilled".
  • Separators. Middots (·) or bullets. No em dashes.
  • Subjects. Use the literal · character, not the HTML entity.

Build

  • Tables only. No flexbox or grid. Outlook ghost table at 580px.
  • Touch targets. CTA buttons at least 44px tall, full width on mobile.
  • Contrast. 4.5:1 minimum for body text, badges and footer text.
  • Icons. Icons8 PNGs at 16–20px. Never OS emojis.
  • Images. Alt text, width and height, data-ogsc and darkreader-ignore.
