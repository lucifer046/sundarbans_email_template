Sundarbans House · Email Design System Standards

All templates in this studio adhere strictly to the AGENTS.md Master Specification developed exclusively for Sundarbans House, IIT Madras BS Degree.

Core Design Directives

  • Branding: Always use "Sundarbans House", "IIT Madras BS Degree", and motto "Learn · Grow · Lead".
  • No OS Emojis: Emojis render inconsistently across Windows, iOS, and Android. Use crisp vector assets or bullet points.
  • No Em Dashes: Never use em dashes. Use middots (·) or hyphens instead.
  • Touch Targets: Minimum 44x44px for all CTA buttons and links.
  • Contrast: Minimum 4.5:1 ratio for body copy against backgrounds.
  • Outlook Centering: Never use flexbox. Use nested <table align="center"> with margin:0 auto.
