Hello Students,
The Technical Department of IIT Madras BS Degree is introducing the Technical Society's DSA Club, a beginner-friendly initiative to help you build strong problem-solving skills and become better prepared for DSA interviews and Competitive Programming.

 
Data Structures & Algorithms
Community · 2026–27
 

The Interview Approach

The club focuses on developing the exact approach expected in technical interviews: understanding the problem statement, identifying edge cases, starting with brute-force solutions, recognising algorithmic patterns, optimising solutions, and confidently explaining and implementing them.

Mastering Problem Solving
01
 Weekly Problem Sets & GSpace Platform You will be added to a dedicated GSpace where you will receive weekly problem sets and have a platform to discuss and share your approaches.
02
 Blogs, Write-ups & Learning Notes The team will share blogs and write-ups, analyse problems, and encourage everyone to stay active and accountable. You are also expected to maintain your own notes or write-ups to document your learning.
03
 Real Platform Contests You will be notified about contests conducted on platforms such as LeetCode, Codeforces, GeeksforGeeks, AtCoder, CodeChef, etc. Rather than conducting separate time-bound contests, the initiative focuses on introducing you to real challenges and building a genuine problem-solving community.
04
 Mock Interviews & Evolving Curriculum Mock interviews will also be conducted based on student interest. The learning plan and problem sets will evolve regularly based on feedback from students and instructors.
