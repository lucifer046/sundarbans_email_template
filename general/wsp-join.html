IITM has updated the region allocation from exam centre city-based to residential city-based. Every student has received a new region allotment via email from Student Affairs. Sundarbans has regional WhatsApp communities where students from the same area connect, collaborate, and coordinate. Follow the steps below to find your new region and join the right group.

How to join your new region
follow the steps based on your situation
1
Find your new region
Search your Gmail for "Student Affairs" or "House Allocation Details". Open the email from student-affairs@study.iitm.ac.in - it mentions your new allocated region (e.g. Lucknow Region). That is your region now.
2
Decide what to do
Choose the option that applies to you
A
Comfortable with the new region
Find your region below, click "Fill Form", submit details, and send the request. We will verify and approve it shortly. You will be added within 24-48 hours.
B
Not comfortable / want to change region
Email Student Affairs (student-affairs@study.iitm.ac.in) and Support Team (support@study.iitm.ac.in) requesting a change. Add me in CC (sundarbans-sec@ds.study.iitm.ac.in). Wait for their reply before joining any group.
3
Already in an old-region group?
If you are comfortable with the new region and are already in the old G-Space or WhatsApp group, please leave the old group first, then fill the form for your new region. Make sure you are not in multiple groups. You can also request a change to the region you currently stay in.
Select Your Region

Choose your region and fill the corresponding form to join your regional WhatsApp community.
