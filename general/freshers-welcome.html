First Things First
Three steps to settle into Sundarbans House
1
Join your regional WhatsApp group

Sundarbans is organized into regional cohorts led by Regional Coordinators. Your regional WhatsApp group link is available directly through the official Sundarbans Lounge portal:

1. Open the Sundarbans Lounge portal and sign in with your official IITM student email ID.
2. Fill in your profile details: enter your real name and your active WhatsApp phone number with country code (+91 …).
3. Scroll down to the bottom of the page to find and tap your allocated regional WhatsApp community group to join.
Open Sundarbans Lounge  →
2
Follow the official WhatsApp broadcast channel

Group chats get active, but the channel carries pure signal: official reminders, session dates, academic slide decks, and house notices without any spam.

Follow Official Channel  →
3
Attend the House Orientation & Q&A

We host an informal orientation for all new students. You will meet your house leads, understand how course registrations and grading work, and get straight answers to common questions about managing workload.

Schedule · Timings and links announced on WhatsApp channel
