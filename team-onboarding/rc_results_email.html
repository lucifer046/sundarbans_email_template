Special Mention

A special shout-out goes to the Chennai and Kolkata regions, where the competition was exceptionally close. We had several outstanding candidates whose interviews showcased remarkable leadership potential, creativity, and dedication. Choosing the final RCs was not an easy decision, and we sincerely appreciate the effort and passion displayed by every participant.

To those who were not selected this time, this is by no means the end of your journey with Sundarbans House. We will soon be expanding our General House Council (GHC), and many enthusiastic and capable candidates will have the opportunity to contribute to the house in meaningful ways. The GHC will also coordinate closely with the RCs in their respective regions to organize meetups and other activities.

Let's work together to make this an incredible term for Sundarbans House. With your support, enthusiasm, and teamwork, let's bring the House Trophy home this year! Once again, congratulations to our newly appointed Regional Coordinators, and thank you to every candidate for your time, effort, and unwavering spirit.

Stay tuned for more updates.
