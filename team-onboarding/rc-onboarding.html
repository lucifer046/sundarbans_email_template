You have been selected as a Regional Coordinator (RC) for the Kolkata region, covering West Bengal, Odisha, & NE states. This is a Position of Responsibility under the Lower House Council, and you will report directly to the Secretary and Deputy Secretary of Sundarbans House.

Regional Coordinator
Term 2026–27

Your Roles and Responsibilities

1.
Query Resolution and Student Support
Act as the primary local representative for hundreds of students across the Kolkata region. Address daily queries on course structures, assignment deadlines, and exam logistics. Help students navigate platform issues, portal workflows, and registration problems by coordinating with TAs and administrative staff.
2.
Communication and Representation
Serve as the official communication bridge between your local student base, the Student Affairs Council, and the central IITM BS program administration. Collect regional feedback, identify recurring systemic issues, and escalate student concerns to program leadership. Ensure all official announcements and academic updates are shared promptly with your region.
3.
Community Engagement and Event Operations
Plan, execute, and host physical meetups and study groups within your region to foster networking and peer collaboration. Work with the PR and Outreach teams to drive local participation in institutional festivals like Paradox. Identify and reach out to potential corporate or academic speakers in your region for House webinars and workshops.
4.
Mentorship and Onboarding
Welcome new student batches from your region and help them adjust to the pace of the online/hybrid model. Build an inclusive local environment that combats student isolation and improves overall well-being within the community.
