Culturals Community Core Team
Term 2026–27 · 07 Oct 2026

As a member of the Culturals Community Core Team, you are the driving force behind Sundarbans House's cultural initiatives. You will work closely with the Culturals Head to execute events, celebrate student talent, and help build a thriving environment for creative expression.

Your Responsibilities
01
 Work closely with the Culturals Head to successfully execute house-level cultural events, festive celebrations, and creative showcases.
02
 Provide direct support, encouragement, and guidance to fellow house members participating in artistic performances, workshops, and competitions.
03
 Actively contribute to planning, coordinating, and managing stage, media, and logistics resources for Sundarbans House cultural initiatives.
04
 Foster a collaborative and inclusive environment that encourages students of all backgrounds and skill levels to engage in creative pursuits.

Someone from the council or your Culturals Head will be in touch over the next few days with onboarding details, including team introductions, the term roadmap, and your initial priorities. Please feel free to reply to this email if you have any questions in the meantime.
