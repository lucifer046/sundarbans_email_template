We are pleased to announce your appointment as the Technical Community Co-heads of Sundarbans House for the term 2026 to 27. This is a meaningful role and we are confident you will carry it with dedication.

Technical Community Co-heads
2026–27

As Technical Community Co-heads, you will be at the centre of Sundarbans House's technical life. You will plan and lead learning initiatives, support fellow students in their technical pursuits, and work alongside the council to make our house a place where curiosity and skill-building thrive.

Your Responsibilities
01
 Mentor students across the house, offer guidance on projects, and help maintain an active and motivated technical community.
02
 Plan and organise workshops, coding sessions, and learning events that are engaging and accessible to students at all levels.
03
 Work toward making Sundarbans House a recognised hub for technical learning, including hackathons, coding circles, and collaborative projects.
04
 Coordinate with the council and other house teams to enable cross-community technical events and collaborative opportunities.

Someone from the council will be in touch over the next few days with onboarding details, including team introductions, the term roadmap, and your initial priorities. Please feel free to reply to this email if you have any questions in the meantime.
